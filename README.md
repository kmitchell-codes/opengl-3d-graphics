# CS330

How do I approach designing software?

I start with a clear picture of what I want the end result to look like, then break it down into smaller, manageable pieces. For this project, that meant identifying each object in my scene and figuring out how to build it using basic shapes.

What new design skills has your work on the project helped you to craft?

This project really improved my ability to take something complex and recreate it using simple components. I also got better at thinking about layout, lighting, and textures as part of the design instead of just afterthoughts.

What design process did you follow for your project work?

I worked from a reference image and started with the most complex object first, then built out the smaller details. From there, it was a lot of adjusting scale, placement, and lighting until everything felt cohesive.

How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?

Breaking things down and working from a reference is something I’ll keep using. It makes bigger projects feel a lot more manageable and keeps things from getting overwhelming.

How do I approach developing programs?

I take development step by step and focus on getting one thing working at a time. I test as I go so I don’t end up with a huge mess to debug later.

What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?

I leaned heavily on trial and error, especially with lighting. I also got better at making small adjustments instead of changing everything at once, which made it easier to see what was actually working.

How did iteration factor into your development?

Iteration was basically the whole process. I kept tweaking objects, lighting, and textures until it looked right. It definitely wasn’t a one-and-done kind of project.

How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones?

At the start, I was just trying to get things to work. By the end, I was more comfortable experimenting, fixing issues, and refining things instead of settling for “good enough.”

How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?

Computer science gives me the skills to actually build things instead of just understanding them. It also helps me think through problems in a more structured way, which is useful in just about anything.

How do computational graphics and visualizations help in your future educational pathway?

They’ve given me a better understanding of how visuals are created and rendered, which will help in future courses related to graphics, game development, and UI design.

How do computational graphics and visualizations help in your future professional pathway?

These skills translate directly into areas like game development, simulation, and interactive design. Knowing how graphics work behind the scenes gives me a solid foundation to build more complex and polished applications later on.
